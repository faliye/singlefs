5. Placing fixed structures in conventional zones would fail due to insufficient space. Conventional zones are typically small (e.g., 4 MiB on many devices), but the root ring placement at r × 8191 × chunk (e.g., 8191 × 4 KiB = 32 MiB for chunk=4 KiB) exceeds typical conventional zone sizes. The product r × 8191 × chunk cannot align with zone boundaries because 8191 is prime and zone sizes are powers of two, making it impossible to fit within the limited conventional zones available.

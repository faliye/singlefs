Destroying S1 frees nothing from N=S2's deadlist. However, if S1 txg=10 and S2 txg=15, and a block with birth=12 is in S2's deadlist (epoch in [10,15)), destroying S1 frees it because birth>5 (prev(S1).txg), but it's referenced by S2 (12≤15).

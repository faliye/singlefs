Q1, does the load check actually earn its keep. Separately, F9 shows that even when a subagent does the ps check correctly and runs alone at the start, the check is only a single snapshot taken once before starting: over a 47-minute run, other sessions kept editing the same tracked files anyway, and a second independent session started the very same heavy stage on its own partway through, neither of which the starting-time ps check could have caught.

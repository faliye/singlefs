1. For G2R: When c exceeds c_max (e.g., c_max=5 but actual c=6 during a publication), df_R overestimates free space. Example: unused=0, freed=0, live metadata=120 blocks. df_R = 0 + 0 + 120 - (10 + 10*5) - (5 + 9*5) = 120 - 60 - 50 = 10. Actual available space is 120 - (10 + 10*6) - 50 = 0. An 8-block write fails despite df_R reporting 10 free blocks. This occurs within 11 publications: after deleting an 8-block object, admission fails, and after 11 publications (including the open window and 10 empty publications), the write still fails due to the reserve miscalculation.

2. For G2S: With 4 slot write failures in one drain (exceeding the simulation's 3-failure limit), the drain requires 12 publications. The bound for G2S is 4 + 2*4 = 12, but the actual need exceeds this. After the disk first became full (excluding the first 3 failures), a drain with 4 slot failures causes the write to fail after 8 publications. Example: ring depth S=4 (N=12), drain starts at txg=100. Four slot write failures occur (txg=100,101,102,103 fail to write, txg=104 succeeds), but the drain needs 12 publications to free space. After 8 publications, the write fails despite df_S calculation.

3. No such variant exists. To reuse blocks freed at txg t after a delete, the reuse bound must be > t. However, maintaining at least 4 distinct candidates requires the oldest candidate's txg to be <= t (since the disk is nearly full and candidate set shrinks to 4 states). Reusing the blocks requires advancing the oldest candidate beyond t, which needs new roots to invalidate older states. But creating new roots requires space, which is unavailable until the freed blocks are reused—creating a circular dependency. Thus, no bounded solution exists without violating the state change constraint.

4. For G2R: With S=1 (N=3), 3 slot write failures during a drain. Each failure forces a retry, requiring 4 publications (txg=10,11,12,13). But the drain needs 14 publications to free space. The bound is 7 + 2*3 = 13, but 14 > 13. For G2S: Smallest N to keep 4 states through a drain burning k extra txgs is N = 3 * ceil((k + 4) / 3). For k=1, N=6; k=3, N=9; k=5, N=9; k=6, N=12.

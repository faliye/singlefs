Context

This is one leg of a three-way check on a file-system crash-consistency verification project called singlefs. A component called layer 0 enumerates every crash state that a recorded write stream could leave on disk. A write barrier (an fsync-like flush point) closes off the segment before it; a crash state is: some prefix of segments fully persisted, one segment where the crash happens has some subset of its own writes persisted, and every later segment has nothing persisted. The baseline (unreduced) formula counts every subset of every segment's own writes. Within a segment a write is either an in-place overwrite (reuses a slot an earlier publish already used) or a copy-on-write (COW) write (lands on a brand-new location). A proposed reduction, called candidate A2 in this project, keeps every subset of a segment's in-place overwrites but restricts that segment's COW writes to only two choices: none of them persisted, or all of them persisted (the one case where the whole segment fully persisted is still counted under the next segment instead, not under this one).

A verdict document written for this project's second round of review reports a small table of five cost figures for one specific write stream (called the second stream, or stream E in one internal debug log): a full-enumeration crash-state count of 5575606380, a measured processing rate of about 13880 states per second, a projected wall-clock time of about 2.3 days on 32 threads, a state count of 210 for candidate A2 on that same stream, and a pair of numbers (262144 total states, 32768 of them flagged red) from one specific 18-write segment used elsewhere in the same verdict to test one condition of a component called the record checker. Your task in this leg is pure arithmetic and consistency checking across the raw facts quoted below: recompute each of the five figures, and for each one state plainly whether it is a directly observed count, a deterministic arithmetic step with no added assumption, or an extrapolation that assumes something beyond what was directly observed. You are not asked to judge whether candidate A2 is an acceptable design choice, only to check the arithmetic and say which steps are extrapolation.

## E75 Record Size and Ring Geometry — Run (2026-09-01, Pure Arithmetic, 16 Unit Tests / All 7 Mutations Caught): **The 4 KiB Settlement Holds Firm, but Its Cited Header Measurement Basis Was Outdated**

[中文](75-记录尺寸与环几何.md) · **English** · [日本語](75-记录尺寸与环几何.ja.md)

D23 (Journal Role and Format) settled item 12 settled record size at 4 KiB, self-acknowledging the basis "**was arithmetic, not empirical measurement**",
while establishing experiment E75 (Record Size and Ring Geometry): under 4 KiB, how many records worst-case transactions consume and the resulting ring size lower bound had never been measured once.

### Criteria (Hardcoded Before Run, Unchanged After Run)

1. **Absolute value assertion**: Number of items accommodated per record must exactly equal `(record_size − record_header) / 56`,
   cell-by-cell equal to arithmetic of E45 (Ring Footprint of Multi-Record Transactions); mismatch indicates one side calculated incorrectly.
2. Under 4 KiB tier, target workload transaction occupies exactly one record (spanning record count = 0).
3. Record header falls entirely within a single `physical_block_size` (I-8.2 (Record Header Does Not Cross Atomic Unit)), verified once each for 512 and 4096.

### ⚠️ Forbidden to Repeat the Mistake of E45 (Ring Footprint of Multi-Record Transactions)

E45 (Ring Footprint of Multi-Record Transactions) implemented I-8.2 (Record Header Does Not Cross Atomic Unit) as "**entire record starts on unit boundary**",
whereas I-8.2 (Record Header Does Not Cross Atomic Unit) literally constrains only the **header**. That fabricated strict rule manufactured a 12× penalty, voiding the conclusion.
E75 (Record Size and Ring Geometry) **implements strictly per literal text**: starting offset `o` is valid ⟺ `o mod unit ≤ unit − hdr`,
pinned by a unit test (twelve 153-byte records literally consume 1836 B, vs 6144 B under strict rule).

### Measurement

whereas the ten fields of settled item 4 totaled **78**, and three settled increments raised it to **95** (current header 487 additionally includes in-publish sequence 4, new root segment 364, fsid 8, and MAC 16, per D23 (Journal Role and Format) settled item 4).

⇒ **If record size reopens to 2 KiB, header measurement basis must be settled prior to calculation.**

#### Criterion 2: Target Workload Occupies Exactly One Record at 4 KiB

12 items (D25 (Target Workload Priority): 8 leaves + 4 ancestors) ÷ 71 items = **1 record, spanning record count 0**, margin **5.92×**.
Holds across all four header measurement bases.

Three measurement bases 78 / 91 / 95 × two pbs tiers 512 and 4096, **all 6 cells `fits=1`**.

#### New Conclusion: Fixed-Length Records + Power-of-2 Sizes ⇒ Alignment Waste of I-8.2 (Record Header Does Not Cross Atomic Unit) Invariably 0

The most expensive cell in the alignment cost table of E23 (Journal Geometry) was **29.3×** (1-item record on 4Kn).
**That table was for variable-length records**: only when record size is not an integer multiple of unit can the subsequent header straddle boundary.
Placing fixed-length 4 KiB (or any power-of-2) records back-to-back, starting offset is invariably an integer multiple of unit
⇒ **header never crosses units, zero bytes of padding appear** (5 sizes × 2 unit tiers across 10 cells, pinned cell-by-cell in unit tests).

⇒ **D23 (Journal Role and Format) settled item 12 adopting fixed-length 4 KiB eliminated the entire alignment dimension as a byproduct** —
the 29.3× of E23 (Journal Geometry) and 12× of E45 (Ring Footprint of Multi-Record Transactions) **are inapplicable to fixed-length records**;
the multiple in E23 (Journal Geometry) was the cost of variable-length records, while that of E45 (Ring Footprint of Multi-Record Transactions) was an artifact of fabricated strict rules.

### What It Cannot Answer

1. Pure arithmetic model: no journal implementation, no transaction layer, 0 file operations.
2. "One item is exactly 56 bytes" and "one 12-item transaction names exactly 12 items" **inherit the measurement basis of E23 (Journal Geometry) / E45 (Ring Footprint of Multi-Record Transactions)**;
   E75 (Record Size and Ring Geometry) does not re-derive them.
3. **Ring wrap-around not modeled** (ring is infinite cursor); **"packing into one record requires buffering entire record in memory first" also not modeled** —
   these two were costs highlighted in debate round of E45 (Ring Footprint of Multi-Record Transactions) pointing clearly against "one record per transaction", remaining unmeasured.
4. The 62 000 item tier is an **extrapolation**: target workload of D25 (Target Workload Priority) is 12 items, that tier has no workload basis.

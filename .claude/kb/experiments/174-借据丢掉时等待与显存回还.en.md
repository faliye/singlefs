### What Was Done

- **Steps for one IOU** (registration Section 5) written as single codebase against `CardHost` trait with two implementations: physical card (`WgpuCardHost`) and simulated card (`SimulatedCardHost`, virtual clock, reading one report records 50 ms, buffers < 256 MiB share a 256 MiB chunk kept once used, larger allocations allocated separately, release requires buffer dropped, submission finished, followed by one poll). Registration Section 9 M1–M7 modify trait-facing code, so card-free tests can turn red. Simulated card reproduces step arithmetic only, not wgpu/driver behavior, not used as readings for G2-B.
- **Subcommands**: `run` (parent process, spawns child processes sequentially per plan, reads entirely after exit, no timestamps in forwarding output loop; resume, refill rounds, compress raw to `.raw.gz`, stops if F1 appears in hold-without-dropping group), `child` (one apparatus process), `judge` (evaluates artifacts, deterministic), `checks` (card-free self-checks, deterministic), `plan` (cell count and child process count per plan stage, deterministic), `simulate` (runs cell several rounds on simulated card, for testing `judge`); `run --simulated` runs children on simulated card, `--limit-jobs N` for smoke testing, `--skip-light` skips in-flight light initially.
- **Plan** (`e174-lease-drop-wait-memory-return-plan-first-2026-10-08.out`, `…-plan-second-2026-10-08.out`): segment one 128 cells, first pass 640 children; segment two 24 cells, first pass 120 children; "hold without dropping" first, in-flight light last.
- **Revision 1**: Registration Section 9 K9 requires e < 0.8 W, while e includes at least U_peak's -q read and B5's compute-apps read (~0.05s each); in-flight light W = 50 ms with 0.8× being only 40 ms, K9 must turn red under registration's own numbers. In-flight light kept per registration and judged per registration; supplemented an "in-flight medium" arm (spin target 250 ms) for each group using in-flight light; voided IOUs no longer read window. Remaining items pin interpretations: definition of valid round, refills starting at 6 up to 3 times, ordering, literal reading of B5, F1 halting, discriminative self-witnessing new τ selection.
- **Judgment method**: `judge --input artifact` groups each cell into "1st IOU" and "2nd/3rd IOU" columns, takes 5 valid rounds with lowest round numbers, lists per round Q1 (wait duration), Q2a / Q2b (first reading after t0 contains buffer), Q2c (duration before excluded), Q3 (retained at window end), Q3b (excess allocation by block), Q3c (wgpu allocator), and emits a `name=verdict` line summarizing F1–F8 and geometric sensitivity; summary fields named "..._ok" represent passing form, `false` indicates non-pass (F2, F3, F4 hits are legitimate registered outcomes, not apparatus faults).

**Question**: compare four arms combining two forms of “accounting item 1 and the reallocation predicate” (甲-T1, G12) with two shadow ledgers (conservative reading, G28), plus the real baseline “today”, in a two-disk mirrored pool. Histories include continuous overwrites by one instance, rollback, retry after root-slot write failure and raising F. Compare counts of states failing I-3.1 (Allocated Statistics Match) / I-5.2 (Free Statistics Match), over-holding (slots the allocator will not issue but nobody references), shadow-ledger isolation, candidate check G27's discriminative power on bad images, and allocation-record write and read amplification.

7. **Continuation (segment four)**: the main agent identified two points from segment three for investigation:

   - **The per-slot cause of G12 failing I-3.1 in rollback families is understood; G12 does so by definition and is not changed.** In H4d2 / s=4 / Impl / period=1 (two red states at txg=48 and 49 in `interval_conservative`), temporary diagnostics, removed after use, checked slot by slot whether records G12 considered occupied but outside the candidate union were actually referenced. Slot 50176 (instance-table unit) had `actually_referenced_by=[37,38]`: it really was referenced by abandoned roots. G12's predicate directly absorbs protection of abandoned-root references into accounting item 1 (the literal “r is abandoned” branch), whereas the registered “within-arm reading” of I-3.1 counts only candidate-set references. These definitions are architecturally misaligned: 甲-T1's main predicate never asks about abandoned-root references and delegates them entirely to a separate shadow ledger; G12 combines them. If abandoned roots have existed and their reference window has not yet been taken over by candidate roots, item 1 necessarily exceeds the candidate union by those slots. Slots 50317–50320 (four records, span 1 each) had `actually_referenced_by=[]`: no root actually referenced them. These are false positives from interval approximation: the rollback target at txg=36 referenced them, but overwrites on abandoned txg=37/38 replaced each with a new slot. Interval `[36,39)` knows a blocking root fell within it, not which slot that root now points to. Registered section-five G12 says Q10 must check whether this inference holds state by state; Q10 was still unimplemented in segment four, but this manual check provided a concrete counterexample early. **Decision fixed: do not change G12's definition; record both causes (overlapping predicate responsibility and interval-approximation false positives), without deciding whether it should be eliminated; that is for three-party inference.**

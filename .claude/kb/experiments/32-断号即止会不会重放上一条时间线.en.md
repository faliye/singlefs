### Assertion (Empirically Confirmed by Rewritten Version, Quantified in "Measured Results")

D23 (Journal Role & Format) itself gave the hole example verbatim as "5849 written, 5850 not written, 5851 written".
Stopping on sequence break was correct for **that specific** recovery: prefix halted at 5849.

**The assertion points out what was missed in the next step**—after recovery, system continues writing from `jsn = 5850`,
while slot 5851 **still holds a record from the previous timeline**, whose checksum is valid and whose `jsn` happens to equal expected.
⇒ Recovery after a second crash may connect and replay it, **even though it belongs to an already-discarded timeline**.

⚠️ **Whether this assertion holds depends on two matters unwritten in this repository**, empirically answered:

| # | Unwritten Matter | How Handled in Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **How slot is determined by `jsn`** (`jsn mod slot_count`? or something else) | **Only tested `jsn mod slot_count`**, the other remains owed |
| 2 | **Where `jsn` resumes writing after truncation** (from prefix end? or skipping entire segment) | **Both were tested** |

⇒ **On Matter 2, "skipping entire segment" does not invalidate the assertion**: it merely shifts the failure direction—
after `jsn` skips, a hole appears between newly written records and applied prefix,
so stopping on sequence break in the next recovery **drops all newly written records**.
**Two natural implementations violate opposite criteria in opposite directions.**

### What Is Measured, Criteria, Failure Clauses (Written Before Running)

- **What is measured**: Sequence of two crashes—first creates hole, recovery truncates, writing resumes, second crash, second recovery;
  count **how many records among replayed belong to discarded timeline**.
- **Criterion**: That count must be **0**.
- **Positive control**: Normal sequence without holes, recovery must drop none and replay no extra—
  otherwise "replayed 0 old timeline records" cannot distinguish safety from not replaying at all.
- **Failure clauses**: If across the **four combinations** of two slot mappings and two `jsn` resumption strategies that count is 0,
  assertion is invalid, **record truthfully**; if any combination is non-zero, that combination is an implementation D23 (Journal Role & Format) must explicitly exclude.
  ⚠️ **Actual run covers 2 / 4** (one slot mapping × two resumption strategies); the other slot mapping remains owed.
- ⚠️ **Must carry absolute assertions** (blocked by absolute-assertions in `.claude/gate.d/code-source-discipline.sh`):
  constructed hole count and discarded timeline record count must be directly provided by construction.

### Measured Results (2026-08-29, N=5 Rounds Byte-identical; Ring 64 Slots, Settled Segment 20 Records, Steady-State Ring)

**Four arms**, differing only in "from which `jsn` writing resumes after recovery" and "whether extra gates exist":

| Arm | Where Writing Resumes | Extra Gate | Replay Discarded Timeline | Drop New Timeline Records | Judgment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `resume_at_prefix` | Prefix end + 1 | None (**Literal form of D23 (Journal Role & Format) settled items**) | **Worst-case = All residuals** | 0 | ✗ |
| `skip_hole` | Max valid `jsn` on ring + 1 | None (requires watermark field nonexistent in repo) | 0 | **Drop all** | ✗ |
| `back_chain` | Prefix end + 1 | Each record carries **hash of previous record** | **0** | **0** | ✓ |
| `erase_then_resume` | Prefix end + 1 | **Physically invalidate** residual slots during recovery | **0** | **0** | ✓ |

**Residuals Replayed by `resume_at_prefix`**:

| Residual Count \ New Records Written After Recovery | 1 | 3 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **1** | 0 | 0 |
| 3 | **3** | **1** | 0 |
| 7 | **7** | **5** | 0 |

⇒ Exact formula (**independently derived by arithmetic, not inferred from tested code**):
`stale_replayed = max(0, residual_count − (new_written_count − 1))`.
Mechanism: Slots on fixed ring determined by `jsn`; each record on new timeline overwrites one residual, and **un-overwritten ones are replayed wholesale**.

**Negative control passed**: Without holes, four arms are cell-by-cell identical (`second_prefix` all `20 + new_written_count`,
both criterion metrics all 0) ⇒ back-chaining does not "reject everything".
**Discriminative power self-proven**: First recovery across all four arms halts at hole (`first_prefix == 20`).

**Three Readings**:

1. **Two natural implementations violate opposite criteria in opposite directions** ⇒ **the gap lies in the specification of D23 (Journal Role & Format), not the implementation**:
   no on-disk field allows implementation to choose correctly between them.
2. **Existing gates verified item-by-item do not trigger**: I-8.3 (Strictly continuous replay prefix) demands `jsn == expected`
   (residuals satisfy it); inflight record limit distinguishes whether **checksum failure** counts as tear or corruption,
   whereas checksum of residual records **is valid**; `checkpoint_txg` within same checkpoint window is identical.
3. **More than two exits exist; measurement covered only two** (adversarial verification added latter two, unmeasured):

   | Exit | Costs Header Bytes | Holds for Zoned Class | Status |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Back-chaining (each carries previous hash) | **Yes** | Holds | E32 (Residuals from Previous Timeline) measured both criteria passed |
   | Physically invalidate residual slots during recovery | No | **Does not hold** (requires in-place overwrite) | E32 (Residuals from Previous Timeline) measured both criteria passed |
   | **Decouple slot from sequence number** (jbd2 variant) | No | Holds | **Unmeasured, used by production implementation** |
   | High bits of sequence number carry journal instance code | Yes, but occupies bits in sequence number | Holds | **Unmeasured** |

### Danger Window

**Danger Window = "Second crash occurs before new timeline completely overwrites residual segment".**
Not an ignorable coincidence: **crashing again immediately after recovery from first crash**
is precisely the class of sequences power loss cycling, failing disks, and crash-point replay harnesses **are designed to enumerate**.
⚠️ **E32 (Residuals from Previous Timeline) did not measure probability under realistic workloads**, only providing its shape and upper bound.

### Measurement Basis and Known Limitations

- Pure logical counting model, no device, no VM. **Steady-state ring**—every slot holds record from previous lap.
- **Slot mapping tested only `jsn mod slot_count`** (covering 2 / 4 failure clause combinations).
  ⚠️ **This dimension is not inherent to "fixed ring", but an uncommitted degree of freedom in this repository**—
  calling `jsn mod slot_count` the "meaning of fixed ring" is an overstatement; implementation comments reflect this measurement basis.
  Auditing jbd2 (local 7.2.0 tree `fs/jbd2/recovery.c` `jbd2_journal_recover` "Restart the log at the next transaction ID"): recovery completion
  `j_transaction_sequence = ++info.end_transaction; j_head = info.head_block;`,
  comment verbatim "Restart the log at the next transaction ID, thus invalidating any existing commit records in the log"
  ⇒ **Sequence number skips break, position rewinds to break, advancing independently**, so residuals mismatch "next expected sequence number at this position".
  ⇒ **The "inevitable replay" measured by E32 (Residuals from Previous Timeline) is inevitable under this specific slot mapping branch, not under the three settled items.**
- **Models only one crash pattern**: one record lost, followed by several landing. Tears, out-of-order landing, multiple holes unmodeled.
- **Does not model checkpoint advancement**: `tail` fixed.
  ⚠️ **However, this gap was independently deduced by two subagents, concluding zero impact**:
  D23 (Journal Role & Format) Settled Item 3 settled "recovery forbidden from trusting tail first",
  and tail advances only after checkpoint persists, while residuals by definition **were never applied**
  ⇒ `tail ≤ min(residual jsn)` holds constantly. Recalculating with tail advanced to `hole + new_written_count`,
  replayed residual count is **cell-by-cell identical** to fixed tail column.
- **`back_chain` hash uses FNV, not cryptographic hash**—E32 (Residuals from Previous Timeline) only requires "different inputs yield different outputs",
  saying nothing about resistance to **adversaries**.

⚠️ **E32 (Residuals from Previous Timeline) twice trapped "checks were dead code"**, caught by mutation testing, both fixed:

1. First version modeled holes as **empty slots**, so sequence break check was never reached (removing it left zero tests red).
   After switching to steady-state ring, removing sequence break check turned **5 tests** red.
2. After removing sequence break check, recovery **accepted infinitely**, causing test process to be OOM-killed,
   which `mutate.sh` then reported as "zero tests failed red"—**misleading developers to modify tests**.
   Added fourth outcome to `mutate.sh` (test process unfinished, reporting signal),
   and added "prefix at most one lap" bound to recovery (literal meaning of "full ring scan").

**Prerequisites**: None. Pure counting model.

**This milestone aims to make four events that never occurred during new-pool file creation happen, and each must be covered by crash-point replay**:
releasing a unit, reusing its placement after a delay, a second writable instance (close and remount), and administrator rollback.
The goal is "correctly committing another transaction in the same pool", not "adding another feature" (`.claude/rules/fs-design.md` "start from transactions, not from features").
